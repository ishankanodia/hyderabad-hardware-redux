FAQs

Wardrobe questions, answered

How much does a modular wardrobe cost in Hyderabad?

It depends on the size and loft height, the type (sliding, hinged or walk-in), the carcass material, the shutter finish and the internal accessories. We measure your bedroom for free and share an itemised quote, so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Sliding or hinged wardrobe: which is better?

Sliding wardrobes save space in compact bedrooms because the doors don't swing out. Hinged wardrobes give full access to the inside and suit most layouts and budgets. Your designer recommends the right option after seeing your room.

Do you design walk-in wardrobes and closets?

Yes. We design open, glass-fronted and closed walk-in wardrobes using Italian Astronea systems. You can walk through real walk-in setups at our Astronea Experience Centre in Srinagar Colony.

Which hardware and wardrobe systems do you use?

Italian Astronea wardrobe systems and aluminium profiles for premium wardrobes, plus branded soft-close hinges, drawers and sliding mechanisms. The brand is named in your quote, and you can test it at our Experience Centre.

Do you make wardrobes in Vijayawada, Vizag and Guntur?

Yes. We take up wardrobe projects across Hyderabad and in major Andhra Pradesh cities including Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Amaravati, Tirupati and Nellore.

Can I get wardrobes with complete home interiors?

Yes. Order wardrobes on their own, or as part of complete home interiors. Complete 3BHK interiors start from ₹3.5 Lakhs* (Standard) and ₹4.5 Lakhs* (Premium). See 3BHK packages.

Is the design consultation really free?

Yes. The design consultation, site measurement and first estimate are free, with no obligation to book.
