FAQs

Questions, answered

How much does 3BHK interior design cost in Hyderabad?

Complete 3BHK interiors with us start from ₹3.5 Lakhs*. Your final cost depends on carpet area, kitchen layout, number and type of wardrobes, materials and finishes, and add-ons like false ceiling, TV or pooja units. You get an itemised quote after a free site measurement.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard starts from ₹3.5 Lakhs* and covers smart, functional interiors for everyday living. Premium starts from ₹4.5 Lakhs* and adds elevated finishes and design details for a more luxurious feel. Your designer helps you choose during the free consultation, and the exact scope, finishes and hardware are confirmed in your itemised quote.

What's the price of a modular kitchen in Hyderabad?

It depends on layout (L-shaped, U-shaped, parallel or island), carcass material, shutter finish and hardware. We quote each module separately, so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Which hardware do you use?

Genuine Blum, Hettich, Salice and Grass for hinges, drawers and lift-ups, plus Astronea for premium wardrobe profiles. The brand is named in your quote, and you can test it at our experience centres.

Can I get only a kitchen or only wardrobes?

Yes. Choose a modular kitchen, wardrobes, or complete home interiors. The ₹3.5 Lakhs* starting price applies to a complete 3BHK package.

Do you work in my city or gated community?

Yes. We work across Hyderabad's apartment communities in Gachibowli, Kokapet, Narsingi, Tellapur, Kondapur, Miyapur, Bachupally and Kompally, and in major Andhra Pradesh cities including Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Amaravati, Tirupati and Nellore. We coordinate with your association for work permits.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The design consultation, site measurement and first estimate are free, with no obligation to book.
